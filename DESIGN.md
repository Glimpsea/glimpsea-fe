# Glimpsea demo

The demo borrows the centered type, italic serif, open space, and staggered photo strip from [Samuel Sanchez’s portfolio](https://samuelsanchez.webflow.io/). The design dials are ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

- Pale Pine #FDEFD6 anchors the page. Deep Burgundy #f31328 fills the order section. Butter Yellow #FEC40b marks the primary action. The 60/30/10 brief guides the visual emphasis as the result grows.
- Inter makes the navigation, order details, and brief easy to scan. Fraunces italic gives the hero and section headings a print editorial voice.
- Original cafe mockups show how a seller could place wall art. Their staggered composition carries the reference's photographic rhythm into the demo.
- The sample order and progress sit together. The brief stays in a reading column. Hover effects support the demo links and button and respect reduced motion.
