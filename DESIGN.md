# Glimpsea demo

The Grunge demo reads like a printed zine for buyer agents. The design dials are ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

- Grain on warm paper and an ink block behind the headline give the opening a photocopied look. Solid paper keeps the order easy to read.
- Permanent Marker sets the headline and wordmark. Special Elite marks section headings. Courier Prime gives order values and the brief the same typewritten voice.
- A taped introduction sits beside the headline. The sample order and progress log follow on one sheet, with a separate column for reading the brief.
- The red button starts the order. Its short tilt responds to hover or press and stops when the reader requests reduced motion.
