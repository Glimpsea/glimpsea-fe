# Glimpsea demo

The demo borrows the centered type, italic serif, and open space from [Samuel Sanchez’s portfolio](https://samuelsanchez.webflow.io/). The design dials are ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

- Pale Pine #FDEFD6 anchors the page. Deep Burgundy #f31328 fills the order section. Butter Yellow #FEC40b marks the primary action. The 60/30/10 brief guides the visual emphasis as the result grows.
- Inter makes the navigation, order details, and brief easy to scan. Fraunces italic gives the hero and section headings a print editorial voice.
- A capture of the extension and labelled sample catalog shows the two-page scan and the report action. The Etsy live checkout receipt sits alongside the walkthrough.
- The simulated buyer-agent order has its own section. Its order and progress sit together, and the brief stays in a reading column. Hover effects respect reduced motion.
